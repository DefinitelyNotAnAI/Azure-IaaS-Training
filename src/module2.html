Module 2 — VNet Peering

Connect your spoke VNet to the hub, enabling cross-spoke traffic inspection and shared services.

What you're about to do

You will create the spoke-side VNet peering that connects your spoke (, ) to the hub VNet (hub-vnet, 10.0.0.0/16).

After you create the spoke-side peering, the instructor will run peer-hub.ps1 to create the matching hub-side peering. Once both sides are Connected, traffic can flow between your spoke and the hub.

  • Add peering on your spoke, pointed at hub-vnet.
  • Leave all access settings at their defaults — only the two Allow access checkboxes need to be checked.
  • Verify peering status reaches Connected after the hub side is added.

How it fits into enterprise Azure

VNet peering is the standard connectivity mechanism between spoke and hub in an Azure Landing Zone:

  • Non-transitive by default — traffic between two spokes doesn't traverse the hub unless routed through the hub firewall via UDRs.
  • Low latency, high throughput — peering traffic stays on the Microsoft backbone and doesn't traverse a VPN gateway.
  • Both sides required — Azure requires a peering resource on each VNet. The platform team (instructor) manages the hub side; you manage the spoke side.
  • Use remote gateway — if the hub has a VPN or ExpressRoute gateway, spokes can use it for on-premises connectivity instead of deploying their own.

Lab steps — Create the spoke-side peering

  1. Open the portal link in the banner above and navigate to your VNet ().
  2. In the left menu, select Peerings under Settings, then click + Add.
  3. The form has two sections. Fill in the Remote virtual network summary section first (this configures the hub side of the peering):
    • Peering link name: — the instructor will see this name on hub-vnet
    • Peering type: Virtual network
    • Subscription: your workshop subscription
    • Virtual network: hub-vnet (hub-rg) — you have Reader access so it appears in the picker
    • Leave all Remote virtual network peering settings at their defaults — only Allow hub-vnet to access your spoke should be checked
  4. Fill in the Local virtual network summary section (this configures your spoke side):
    • Peering link name:
    • Leave all Local virtual network peering settings at their defaults — only Allow your spoke to access hub-vnet should be checked
  5. Click Add. The peering will show Initiated until the instructor adds the hub side.
  6. Raise your hand or message in the group chat to let the instructor know you're ready for the hub-side peering.
  7. Once the instructor runs peer-hub.ps1, refresh the Peerings blade. Status should change to Connected.
