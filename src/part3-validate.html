Verify in your data

If your agent missed the root cause, use this KQL snippet to find it directly in your Eventhouse — then compare what you found to what the agent returned:

// Find the planted incident directly
SlotTelemetry_<>()
| where IncidentId != ''
| summarize Count = count(), AvgLatencyMs = avg(LatencyMs),
    FirstSeen = min(Timestamp), LastSeen = max(Timestamp)
    by IncidentId, Service, IsAnomaly
| order by FirstSeen asc
// Which tickets were tagged with that IncidentId?
SlotTickets_<>()
| where IncidentId != ''
| summarize TicketCount = count(),
    Tenants = make_set(CustomerTenant),
    FirstTicket = min(Timestamp)
    by IncidentId
| order by FirstTicket asc

Stretch — Recommend remediation

Now that your agent (and you) know what happened, send this final prompt:

"Based on everything you've found — the root cause, the affected customers, and the blind spot in monitoring — what are your top 3 recommended actions for the application owner to take right now and to prevent recurrence?"

The agent cannot execute these actions (read-only by design), but its recommendations should be specific and grounded in the data it retrieved — not generic advice. Good recommendations will reference the specific service, the monitoring gap, and the customer impact tier.
