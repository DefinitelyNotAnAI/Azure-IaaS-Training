What your app is generating

  • Telemetry (Plane A): per-operation latency, error rate, throughput — high-frequency, time-series data landing in the shared Eventhouse.
  • Support tickets (Plane B): simulated customer complaints reacting to system state — unstructured text landing in the shared Lakehouse.
  • Both streams carry correlation dimensions: slotId, region, service, feature, timestamp — the fields Part 2's KQL queries will join on.

The chaos engine will inject incidents on a shared timeline. Watch for a latency/error spike and a corresponding burst of "slow system" tickets — that's the planted root cause your Part 3 agent will need to find.
