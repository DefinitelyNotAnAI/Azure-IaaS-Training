An Azure Landing Zone is Microsoft's prescriptive approach to building a secure, scalable, well-governed Azure estate. It separates platform concerns from workload concerns:

  • A central hub VNet carries shared services — firewall, DNS resolver, VPN/ExpressRoute gateway. Managed by the platform team.
  • Each application team deploys into a spoke VNet with a dedicated, non-overlapping CIDR, peered back to the hub.
  • Azure Policy enforces configuration standards automatically across the estate — no per-resource manual governance.
  • RBAC scopes each team's access to their own spoke — Contributor on their RG, Reader on the hub (to peer), nothing else.

Today you are the application team. Your resource group () is your spoke. In Module 2 you'll request the platform team (your instructor) to peer your spoke to the hub.
